# Deep Dives

A collection of articles, experiments, and projects spanning statistics, machine learning, economics, mathematics, and other fields that catch my curiosity. Some pieces are more discursive, some more computational, others more mathematical—but each tries to maintain an applied approach to make explanations clearer.

> *"It is not our part to master all the tides of the world, but to do what is in us [...] uprooting the evil in the fields that we know, so that those who live after may have clean earth to till."* — J.R.R. Tolkien

Sharing these notes and striving for clarity is simply my own way of trying to leave behind a clean, cultivated earth for whoever comes next.
