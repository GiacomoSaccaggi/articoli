# Deep Dives

> *"If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough."* — Albert Einstein

Welcome to **Deep Dives**—a personal repository of articles, computational experiments, and technical notes spanning statistics, machine learning, mathematics, economics, and other fields that pique my curiosity.

---

## 🎯 About This Repository

Some topics explored here are more discursive, others are deeply mathematical or computational. Regardless of the domain, every piece strives to maintain a practical, hands-on approach to make complex explanations clear and accessible.

> *"It is not our part to master all the tides of the world, but to do what is in us [...] uprooting the evil in the fields that we know, so that those who live after may have clean earth to till."* — J.R.R. Tolkien

Sharing these notes and aiming for clarity is my way of documenting what I learn—and hopefully leaving behind a clean, cultivated space for anyone curious enough to explore.

---

## 🗺️ Areas Covered

* **Statistics & Data Science:** Resampling, non-parametric methods, Bayesian analysis, time series, and causal inference.
* **Machine Learning & MLOps:** Supervised & unsupervised learning, deep learning architectures, anomaly detection, model serving, and GPU workflows.
* **Mathematics:** State-space models, Markov chains, Monte Carlo methods, and network analysis.
* **Economics:** Quantitative analysis, economic thought, and market dynamics.
* **Software Engineering & Tooling:** Python internals, package development, databases, and custom automation.
